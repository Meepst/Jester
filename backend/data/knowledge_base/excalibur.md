# Excalibur Hotel & Casino Las Vegas — Knowledge Base

## Overview
Excalibur Hotel & Casino is one of the most recognizable resorts on the Las Vegas Strip, designed to look like a massive medieval castle complete with turrets, drawbridge, and colorful towers. Located at 3850 Las Vegas Blvd South, it opened in 1990 and was the largest hotel in the world at the time. Today it remains one of the best-value resorts on the Strip, beloved for its family-friendly atmosphere, fun theming, and unbeatable price point.

## The Castle
- The exterior is styled as a classic European medieval castle with white and red-tipped towers, battlements, and a moat.
- The drawbridge entrance sets the theatrical tone immediately upon arrival.
- Inside, the medieval theme continues with heraldic banners, knights in armor, stained-glass motifs, and castle-inspired décor throughout.
- The property is enormous — over 100,000 square feet of casino floor and nearly 4,000 rooms.

## Hotel Rooms & Suites
- Approximately 3,981 rooms and suites — one of the largest room counts in Las Vegas.
- **Standard Rooms** — Comfortable, recently renovated rooms at exceptional value. One of the best prices per night among Strip-connected hotels.
- **Deluxe Rooms** — Slightly larger with upgraded furnishings.
- **Corner Suites** — Panoramic views of the Strip and surrounding Las Vegas valley.
- **Royal Tower Suites** — Premium suite experience with separate living areas.
- Excellent choice for large groups, families, or anyone who wants a Strip address without a Strip price.

## Casino
- One of the largest casino floors in Las Vegas at approximately 100,000 square feet.
- **Slots & Video Poker** — Massive selection of machines. Competitive pay tables for a Strip property.
- **Table Games** — Blackjack, craps, roulette, baccarat, three-card poker, Let It Ride, and more.
- **Poker Room** — Active poker room with regular cash games and tournaments.
- **Race & Sports Book** — Modern facility with large screens for watching games.
- Known as a more relaxed casino environment — less intimidating for newer gamblers.
- Fun, lively atmosphere that matches the festive castle theme.

## Dining
- **Buca di Beppo** — Italian-American family-style dining with generous portions. Perfect for groups.
- **The Steakhouse at Camelot** — Signature fine dining restaurant in a candlelit castle-chamber setting. Excellent steaks and an impressive wine list. One of the hidden gems among Strip steakhouses.
- **Camelot Buffet** — Hearty buffet with comfort food and rotating stations.
- **Sherwood Forest Café** — 24-hour casual dining for any time of day or night.
- **Red Sushi** — Japanese cuisine and sushi bar with creative rolls.
- **Excalibur Food Court** — Multiple quick-service options for fast, affordable meals. Great for families.
- **Castle Bar** — Central bar in the casino for drinks and casual fun.

## Entertainment
- **Tournament of Kings** — The crown jewel of Excalibur's entertainment. A two-hour medieval dinner show featuring jousting knights on real horses, sorcerers, dragons, sword fights, and acrobatics — all while you eat a four-course feast with your hands (no utensils — eat like royalty!). One of the longest-running dinner theater shows in Las Vegas history. Audience members cheer for their assigned kingdom's knight. Incredibly fun for all ages.
- **Thunder From Down Under** — Long-running male revue show featuring the "Australian Hunks." One of the most popular shows in Las Vegas.
- **Lil' Baja** — Fun entertainment lounge with live acts.
- Regular live music in casino bars and lounge areas.

## The Fun Dungeon (Arcade & Entertainment)
- **The Fun Dungeon** — Large arcade and midway games area on the lower level. Carnival-style games, redemption arcade machines, prizes, and activities. Perfect for younger guests or anyone who loves a good midway challenge.
- One of the few casino properties on the Strip that still has a dedicated arcade area.

## Pool
- Outdoor pool complex open seasonally.
- Tropical-themed pool area with a relaxed atmosphere.
- Pool bar service during operating hours.
- Family-friendly environment compared to many party-focused Strip pools.

## Spa & Fitness
- **Spa & Salon at Excalibur** — Full-service spa offering massages, body treatments, facials, and salon services.
- Fitness center available to hotel guests with cardio and weight equipment.

## Shopping
- **Medieval Village** — Shopping area on the lower level with specialty stores, gifts, souvenirs, and artisan goods with a medieval/fantasy theme.
- Logo merchandise, knight-themed gifts, and unique Las Vegas souvenirs.

## Connections & Location
- Located at 3850 Las Vegas Blvd South, southern Strip.
- Connected to **Luxor** to the south via free tram and indoor walkway.
- Connected to **Mandalay Bay** via Luxor — all three properties form a walkable campus.
- Walking distance to MGM Grand, New York-New York, and Park MGM.
- The **Las Vegas Monorail** connects nearby.
- Approximately 3.5 miles from Harry Reid International Airport — one of the most convenient Strip locations relative to the airport.
- Free self-parking available.

- **Tournament of Kings** — A genuinely unique, unforgettable experience that no other Vegas property can replicate.

## Excalibur in Las Vegas History
- Opened June 19, 1990 — was the largest hotel in the world upon opening.
- Built by Circus Circus Enterprises (now MGM Resorts International).
- Was part of a wave of family-friendly mega-resorts that transformed the Strip in the early 1990s, alongside the Luxor and Treasure Island.
- Has hosted countless family vacations, bachelor/bachelorette parties, and first-time Las Vegas visitors.
- The castle silhouette is one of the most photographed and recognizable on the entire Strip.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the Tournament of Kings?**
It's a two-hour medieval dinner show with jousting on real horses, sword fights, acrobatics, a dragon, and sorcery — all while you eat a four-course feast with your hands. Guests are assigned to a kingdom and cheer for their knight throughout the show. It runs multiple nights per week and is one of the most unique entertainment experiences in Las Vegas.

**Is Excalibur good for families?**
Yes — it's one of the most family-friendly resorts on the Strip. The castle theme appeals to kids, the Fun Dungeon arcade is great for older children and teens, Tournament of Kings is appropriate for all ages, and the room rates make it financially accessible for family trips.

**Is Excalibur connected to other hotels?**
Yes — Excalibur is connected to the Luxor via a free tram and indoor walkway. From Luxor you can walk to Mandalay Bay. This gives guests access to three distinct resort experiences without going outside.

**How are the rooms for the price?**
Excalibur rooms are consistently rated as good value — clean, comfortable, and functional. They won't match the ultra-luxury of Wynn or Bellagio, but for the price point (often among the lowest on the Strip), they are excellent. Recently renovated rooms are particularly well-regarded.

**What are the show times for Tournament of Kings?**
Shows typically run Wednesday through Sunday with evening performances. Times vary — check the Excalibur website or the concierge for the current schedule as it changes seasonally.

**What is check-in/check-out time?**
Check-in is at 3:00 PM. Check-out is at 11:00 AM.

**Is there a resort fee?**
Like most Las Vegas Strip properties, Excalibur charges a nightly resort fee that covers amenities like Wi-Fi, pool access, and fitness center use. Confirm the current fee at time of booking.
