# Luxor Las Vegas — Knowledge Base

## Overview
The Luxor Las Vegas is one of the most recognizable hotels in the world, shaped as a massive 30-story black glass pyramid rising 350 feet above the Strip. Located at 3900 Las Vegas Blvd South, it opened in 1993. The iconic **Sky Beam** — the strongest beam of light in the world — shoots from the pyramid's apex 10 miles into the night sky and is visible from aircraft as far away as Los Angeles.

## The Pyramid & Architecture
- The pyramid contains 4,408 rooms and suites on its inclined walls.
- Elevators called **inclinators** travel at a 39-degree angle along the interior walls of the pyramid.
- The interior atrium is one of the largest in the world at 29 million cubic feet.
- The Sphinx at the entrance is 10 stories tall — larger than the original in Egypt.
- The pyramid's exterior is coated in dark glass, giving it a mysterious black silhouette day and night.
- Two additional towers (Luxor Tower North and South) were added later and contain conventional rooms.

## The Sky Beam
- The Sky Beam is produced by 39 xenon lamps, each with a 7,000-watt bulb.
- On clear nights the beam is visible from over 275 miles away.
- Moths and insects are attracted to the beam in massive numbers, which in turn attracts bats — creating a unique nightly ecosystem at the apex.
- The beam costs approximately $51 per hour to operate.

## Hotel Rooms & Suites
- **Pyramid Rooms** — Unique wedge-shaped rooms on the inclined pyramid walls. Windows look straight down to the atrium floor far below.
- **Tower Rooms** — Standard hotel room layout in the north and south towers.
- **Suites** — Multiple suite categories including spa suites and luxury corner suites with panoramic Strip views.
- Total of approximately 4,400 rooms across the pyramid and towers.

## Casino
- Casino floor spans approximately 120,000 square feet.
- Games include slots, blackjack, craps, roulette, baccarat, poker, and a race & sports book.
- The **Luxor Poker Room** is a popular spot for both casual and competitive players.
- High-limit gaming areas available for serious players.

## Dining
- **Tender Steakhouse** — Signature steakhouse known for prime cuts and an extensive wine list.
- **More** — Buffet-style dining with a wide variety of options.
- **Public House** — Gastropub with craft beers, cocktails, and elevated bar food.
- **Backstage Deli** — New York-style deli open 24 hours; a Vegas institution for late-night sandwiches.
- **Tacos & Tequila (T&T)** — Lively Mexican restaurant and bar.
- **Food court** — Multiple casual dining options for quick meals.

## Entertainment & Attractions
- **Luxor IMAX Theatre** — One of the largest IMAX screens in the world, showing blockbuster films.
- **Bodies: The Exhibition** — Fascinating anatomical exhibition displaying preserved human bodies and specimens. Educational and thought-provoking.
- **Titanic: The Artifact Exhibition** — Over 300 authentic artifacts recovered from the RMS Titanic wreck site, plus a full-scale recreation of the ship's grand staircase.
- **Fantasy** — Long-running adult revue show featuring elaborate costumes and stunning performers.
- **Carrot Top** — Legendary prop comedian with a long-running residency at the Luxor; one of Las Vegas's most beloved and unique acts.

- Multiple bars and lounges throughout the property.

## Spa & Fitness

## Shopping

## Location & Neighbors
- Located at the southern end of the Strip at 3900 Las Vegas Blvd South.
- Connected via indoor walkway to **Mandalay Bay** to the south and **Excalibur** to the north via covered pedestrian bridge and tram.
- The three properties (Mandalay Bay, Luxor, Excalibur) form a connected campus — guests can walk between all three without going outside.
- Approximately 3.5 miles from Harry Reid International Airport — one of the closest major resorts to the airport.

## Egyptian Mythology & Theme
Amara the oracle draws on rich Egyptian mythology woven throughout the property:
- **Ra** — The sun god; the Sky Beam symbolizes Ra's light illuminating the world.
- **Osiris** — God of the afterlife and resurrection; the pyramid shape is sacred to Osiris.
- **Horus** — Falcon-headed sky god; the Eye of Horus motif appears throughout the decor.
- **Isis** — Goddess of magic and wisdom.
- **The Sphinx** — Guardian and protector; the Luxor Sphinx watches over all who enter.
- **Hieroglyphs** — Decorative hieroglyphic motifs are used throughout the property's design.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What makes the pyramid rooms unique?**
Pyramid rooms have angled walls and windows that look straight down into the massive atrium. The inclinators (diagonal elevators) are a one-of-a-kind experience. It's an architecture unlike any other hotel room in the world.

**How do I get to the upper pyramid floors?**
Via the inclinators — angled elevators that travel along the 39-degree slope of the pyramid walls.

**Can I visit the exhibitions without staying at the hotel?**
Yes. Bodies and Titanic exhibitions are open to the public. Tickets can be purchased at the box office or online.

**Is the Sky Beam visible every night?**
Yes, the Sky Beam operates every night after dark. It is most visible on clear nights with no cloud cover.

**What is check-in/check-out time?**
Check-in is at 3:00 PM, check-out is at 11:00 AM.

**Is the Luxor connected to other hotels?**
Yes — the Luxor is connected to Excalibur via a free tram and indoor walkway, and to Mandalay Bay via indoor walkway, making it easy to explore all three properties.
