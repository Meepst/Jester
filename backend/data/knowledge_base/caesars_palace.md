# Caesars Palace Las Vegas — Knowledge Base

## Overview
Caesars Palace is one of the most iconic luxury resort-casinos on the Las Vegas Strip, located at 3570 Las Vegas Blvd South. Opened in 1966, it was designed to evoke the grandeur of ancient Rome. The property spans 85 acres and features multiple hotel towers, a world-class casino, fine dining, luxury shopping, and a legendary entertainment venue.

## Hotel Towers & Rooms
Caesars Palace has six hotel towers:
- **Augustus Tower** — Classic Roman-themed rooms, most recently renovated. Known for spacious layouts.
- **Palace Tower** — The original tower, centrally located. Mix of classic and updated rooms.
- **Julius Tower** — Modern rooms with Strip or pool views.
- **Octavius Tower** — Opened 2012. Contemporary rooms with floor-to-ceiling windows.
- **Forum Tower** — Connected to the Forum Shops. Convenient for shoppers.
- **Nobu Hotel at Caesars Palace** — Boutique hotel-within-a-hotel experience with Japanese-inspired design. Exclusive and intimate.

Room types range from Deluxe Rooms to Suites, including the legendary 5,000-square-foot Sky Villa suites with private pools.

## Casino
- One of the largest casinos in Las Vegas at approximately 124,181 square feet.
- Games: slots, video poker, blackjack, baccarat, craps, roulette, poker, and high-limit rooms.
- The **Baccarat Pit** is world-famous and attracts high rollers from around the globe.
- **Caesars Race & Sports Book** — one of the most elaborate in Las Vegas.
- High-limit gaming areas offer exclusivity and personalized service.

## Dining
- **Gordon Ramsay Hell's Kitchen** — The celebrity chef's Las Vegas flagship; signature dishes from the TV show.
- **Gordon Ramsay Pub & Grill** — Casual British pub fare with a lively atmosphere.
- **Nobu Restaurant** — World-renowned Japanese-Peruvian fusion by Nobu Matsuhisa.
- **Restaurant Guy Savoy** — Three Michelin-starred French cuisine; one of the finest restaurants in the US.
- **MR CHOW** — Iconic fine Chinese dining with theatrical tableside service.
- **Bacchanal Buffet** — Award-winning buffet widely considered the best in Las Vegas; 500+ menu items.
- **Old Homestead Steakhouse** — Classic American steakhouse with premium dry-aged beef.
- **The Cheesecake Factory** — Casual dining option inside the Forum Shops.

## Entertainment & Nightlife
- **Colosseum at Caesars Palace** — 4,300-seat world-class concert and entertainment venue. Home to legendary residencies by Celine Dion, Elton John, Adele, Mariah Carey, and many more.
- **OMNIA Nightclub** — Premier nightclub with cutting-edge production and top DJ residencies.
- **Cleopatra's Barge** — Iconic floating lounge and nightclub, a Caesars institution since 1974.

## Pool & Spa
- **Garden of the Gods Pool Oasis** — Seven pools and three whirlpools spread across 4.5 acres of Roman-inspired gardens. Open year-round.
- Pool options include the Apollo Pool, Venus Pool (adults-only), Neptune Pool, and the Jupiter Pool.
- **Qua Baths & Spa** — 50,000-square-foot spa facility featuring Roman baths, arctic ice room, dry sauna, and over 50 treatment rooms.

## Shopping
- **The Forum Shops at Caesars** — 160+ stores across 636,000 square feet. One of the highest-grossing malls in the US. Features luxury brands like Versace, Fendi, Louis Vuitton, Harry Winston, and dozens more. The famous spiral escalator and talking statues are iconic photo spots.

## Loyalty & Rewards
- **Caesars Rewards** (formerly Total Rewards) — One of the most recognized loyalty programs in gaming. Members earn credits on gaming, hotel stays, dining, and entertainment. Tier levels: Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Diamond Plus, Diamond Elite, Seven Stars.

## Special Features & Fun Facts
- The famous **Fountains of Bellagio** are visible from Caesars, though the fountains belong to Bellagio next door.
- The hotel has hosted some of the most famous boxing matches in history, including multiple Mike Tyson fights.
- Caesars Palace has appeared in countless films and TV shows, including The Hangover, Rain Man, Rocky IV, and Ocean's Eleven.
- Over 3,900 rooms and suites total.
- Employs over 8,000 people.
- The iconic **SPQR** (Senatus Populusque Romanus) motto adorns the property throughout.

## Location & Transportation
- Located mid-Strip at 3570 Las Vegas Blvd South.
- Neighbors: Bellagio (south), The Mirage (north), Paris Las Vegas (across the street).
- Self-parking and valet available.
- Walking distance to most major Strip attractions.
- Approximately 5 miles from Harry Reid International Airport.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the check-in time at Caesars Palace?**
Check-in is at 4:00 PM. Check-out is at 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out may be available for Caesars Rewards members or for an additional fee.

**Does Caesars Palace have a dress code?**
Smart casual is recommended throughout most of the property. OMNIA Nightclub enforces a strict dress code — no athletic wear, flip flops, or overly casual attire.

**Is the pool heated?**
Yes, the Garden of the Gods pools are heated year-round.

**Can non-guests visit the Forum Shops?**
Yes, the Forum Shops are open to the public. No hotel stay is required.

**Does Caesars offer airport transfers?**
Yes, car service and limousine transfers can be arranged through the concierge.
