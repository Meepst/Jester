# Santa Fe Station Hotel & Casino — Knowledge Base

## Overview
Santa Fe Station Hotel & Casino is a beloved locals' casino located at 4949 N Rancho Drive in northwest Las Vegas, far from the tourist bustle of the Strip. Opened in 1991 (originally as Santa Fe Hotel & Casino), it was purchased by Station Casinos and has become one of the most popular destinations for Las Vegas residents. Known for its relaxed, unpretentious atmosphere, great value, and genuine community feel.

## The Station Casinos Family
Santa Fe Station is part of the **Station Casinos** family — the dominant locals casino operator in Las Vegas. Other Station properties include Red Rock Casino, Green Valley Ranch, Palms (managed), Boulder Station, Sunset Station, and more. Station Casinos is known for treating locals exceptionally well with generous promotions, great food, and comfortable, no-frills fun.

## Casino
- Casino floor with a wide variety of gaming options.
- **Slots & Video Poker** — Huge selection; locals know Santa Fe for loose video poker machines with great pay tables. Full-pay Deuces Wild and other favorable games can often be found.
- **Table Games** — Blackjack, craps, roulette, baccarat, and three-card poker.
- **Poker Room** — Active poker room with regular tournaments and cash games. Friendly to all skill levels.
- **Race & Sports Book** — Comfortable, well-equipped sports book with big screens. Popular on game days.
- Known for **generous slot club promotions** and frequent drawings and giveaways for locals.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Santa Fe Station a good place to stay if I'm visiting the Strip?**
It's a great option if you want to save money and experience Las Vegas like a local. It's about 15-20 minutes from the Strip and much more affordable. Many visitors use it as a home base and Uber/Lyft to the Strip for sightseeing.

**Does Santa Fe Station have good video poker?**
Yes — it's one of the reasons locals love it. Look for full-pay Deuces Wild, 9/6 Jacks or Better, and other favorable pay tables that are harder to find on the Strip.

**Is the bowling alley open late?**
Yes, the bowling center typically has late-night hours, especially on weekends. Check the current schedule as hours can vary.

**Do I need a reservation at The Oyster Bar?**
The Oyster Bar operates on a first-come, first-served basis. It can get busy, especially on weekends. Arriving early or during off-peak hours is recommended.

**What's the Boarding Pass program?**
It's the Station Casinos loyalty card — free to sign up at any Station property. You earn points every time you play, dine, or stay. Points translate to free play, meals, and more. Highly recommended for anyone visiting more than once.

**Is there an ice skating rink?**
Yes! Santa Fe Station has a full ice skating rink — one of the only casino properties in Las Vegas to offer this. It's a great option, especially for families or visitors looking for something different.
