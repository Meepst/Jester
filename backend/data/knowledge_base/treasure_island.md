# Treasure Island (TI) Las Vegas — Knowledge Base

## Overview
Treasure Island, commonly known as **TI**, is a resort-casino located at 3300 Las Vegas Blvd South on the northern end of the Strip. Opened in 1993 by Steve Wynn as a pirate-themed resort, it has since evolved into a more modern, adult-oriented destination while retaining its swashbuckling charm. TI is known for being approachable, fun, and an excellent value on the Strip — friendlier to the wallet than many of its neighbors.

## The Pirate Heritage
When it opened, TI featured the legendary **Buccaneer Bay** free outdoor pirate battle show — a massive spectacle with a full pirate ship and a British Royal Navy frigate that played out dramatic battles every 90 minutes. The show was one of the most popular free attractions in Las Vegas history and ran for years before being retired. The swashbuckling spirit lives on in TI's identity and Captain Morgan's persona.

## Hotel Rooms & Suites
- Approximately 2,884 rooms and suites in a single 36-story tower.
- **Standard Rooms** — Spacious by Las Vegas standards, renovated with contemporary décor. Views of the Strip or pool courtyard.
- **Petite Suites** — Step-up option with a separate sitting area.
- **Tower Suites** — Upper-floor suites with sweeping Strip views.
- **Penthouse Suites** — Premium accommodations with luxury furnishings and panoramic views.
- All rooms feature in-room safes, flat-screen TVs, and complimentary Wi-Fi.
- Excellent value compared to neighboring Strip resorts of similar quality.

## Casino
- Casino floor spans approximately 95,000 square feet.
- **Slots & Video Poker** — Wide variety of machines with competitive pay tables.
- **Table Games** — Blackjack (including single-deck and double-deck games), craps, roulette, baccarat, three-card poker, Let It Ride, and more.
- **Poker Room** — Active poker room with cash games and tournaments.
- **Race & Sports Book** — Modern sports book with multiple large screens.
- Known for having some of the **best blackjack rules on the Strip** — single-deck and double-deck blackjack with favorable conditions.
- High-limit gaming room for serious players.

## Dining
- **Gilley's Saloon, Dance Hall & Bar-B-Que** — The signature restaurant. Texas-style BBQ, live country music, a mechanical bull, and a lively bar scene. One of the most entertaining dining experiences on the Strip. Boot-scootin' good time.
- **Phil's Italian & Steak House** — Upscale casual Italian-American dining with great steaks and handmade pasta.
- **Starbucks** — Located in the casino for your morning brew.
- **Canter's Deli** — New York-style deli with sandwiches, soups, and 24-hour comfort food — a Vegas institution originally from Los Angeles.
- **The Wok** — Asian cuisine with a variety of dishes.
- **Breeze Snack Bar** — Poolside snacks and light bites.
- Food court options for casual, affordable meals.

## Entertainment & Nightlife
- **Mystère by Cirque du Soleil** — TI's crown jewel. One of the original Cirque du Soleil shows on the Strip and widely regarded as one of the best. A timeless, stunning spectacle of acrobatics, aerial feats, comedy, and breathtaking theatrics. Has performed at TI since 1993.
- **Señor Frog's Las Vegas** — Legendary party bar and restaurant known for outrageous fun, party drinks, and nonstop entertainment. One of the liveliest spots on the Strip.
- **Gilley's** doubles as a nightlife destination with live country music, dancing, and the mechanical bull.
- Casino bar and multiple lounge areas for relaxed drinks.

## Pool
- **Buccaneer Bay Pool** — Outdoor pool area with a tropical, nautical theme befitting the pirate spirit.
- Heated pool open seasonally.
- Pool bar service during operating hours.
- Relaxed, less crowded than many Strip resort pools.

## Spa & Fitness
- **Tattoo'd Lady Spa** — Full-service spa with a edgy, rock-and-roll aesthetic. Massages, facials, body wraps, and nail services.
- Fitness center with cardio and weight equipment available to hotel guests.

## Shopping
- **TI Fashion** — Retail shops with clothing, accessories, and gifts.
- Logo merchandise and pirate-themed souvenirs.
- Walkway to The Venetian and Palazzo connected via pedestrian bridge over the Strip, giving easy access to the Grand Canal Shoppes.

## Location & Neighbors
- Located at 3300 Las Vegas Blvd South, northern Strip.
- Neighbors: **The Venetian** and **Palazzo** directly across the street (connected via pedestrian bridge/walkway). **Wynn Las Vegas** just to the north. **The Mirage** (now under renovation/transition) to the south.
- Central Strip location makes it easy to walk to most major attractions.
- Approximately 4 miles from Harry Reid International Airport.
- Free self-parking available in the parking garage.

## Value Proposition
TI is frequently cited as one of the best values on the main Strip:
- More affordable room rates than neighboring Venetian or Wynn.
- Excellent blackjack conditions attract savvy gamblers.
- Mystère by Cirque du Soleil is world-class entertainment at competitive ticket prices.
- Free parking always available.
- Central Strip location without the premium price tag of ultra-luxury neighbors.

## Blackjack — Why Gamblers Love TI
- TI is known among blackjack players for offering **single-deck and double-deck blackjack** with relatively player-friendly rules.
- Lower house edge compared to many Strip competitors.
- Attracts skilled players and those who appreciate favorable game conditions.
- Always confirm current rules at the table as conditions can change.

## Mystère by Cirque du Soleil
- Running since 1993 — one of the longest-running shows in Las Vegas history.
- Performed in a custom-built 1,541-seat theater designed specifically for the show.
- Features jaw-dropping aerial acrobatics, bungee artistry, contortionists, giant babyheads, and the unforgettable Hand-to-Hand act.
- Suitable for all ages — family-friendly while being genuinely spectacular for adults.
- Tickets typically range from $75–$130+. Discount tickets sometimes available for Mlife/MGM rewards members (note: TI is not MGM, always check current affiliations).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is TI a good value on the Strip?**
Absolutely. TI consistently offers some of the best room rates among major Strip resorts in its location. Combined with free parking, good casino odds, and world-class entertainment in Mystère, it's a favorite among value-conscious visitors who don't want to sacrifice location or quality.

**Does TI still have the pirate show?**
The original outdoor Buccaneer Bay pirate battle show was retired, but the pirate spirit lives on in TI's identity, décor, and Captain Morgan's legendary persona.

**What is Mystère?**
Mystère is Cirque du Soleil's resident show at TI and one of the most acclaimed circus-arts shows in the world. It has been performing at TI since 1993 and is considered essential Las Vegas viewing.

**What are TI's blackjack rules?**
TI is well-known for offering single-deck and double-deck blackjack games. Always verify current rules (blackjack pays, dealer hit/stand on soft 17, doubling rules) at the table upon sitting down.

**Is Gilley's really worth visiting?**
Yes! Gilley's is a genuine experience — BBQ, live music, mechanical bull riding, and a rowdy country bar atmosphere. Even if country music isn't your thing, the energy is infectious and the food is solid.

**What is the check-in time?**
Check-in is at 3:00 PM. Check-out is at 11:00 AM.

**Is free parking really free?**
Yes — TI offers complimentary self-parking in their parking garage, which is increasingly rare on the Strip and a significant perk for visitors.
